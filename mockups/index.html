Hi Sam, sorry about that. I can see the duplicate charge on your March invoice and I've flagged it for a refund, you should see it within 3–5 business days. Let me know if anything else comes up.
Approve & send is the only path a message reaches the customer through. There's no agent-facing action that skips this screen, that's the human-in-the-loop gate the PRD describes.
